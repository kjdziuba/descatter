## Context
- **E1 as specified can't show the confusion H1 is about.** watford's
  degeneracy check (`results/e1/degeneracy_review.json`, 138265b) found:
  - at tissue SNR (~295), the α truth and the whole β family (a′, n∞′, φ′,
    amplitude and linear terms profiled) are 4×10⁴–7×10⁵ χ² apart, at every
    radius, in both conditions;
  - the confusion appears only at Amide I SNR ≈ 1–4.
- **Chris's decision (2026-11-06).** Run E1's grid as written, with the
  prediction already recorded (raw H1 passes, preprocessed H1 fails). Then
  design E1b. See `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`: the E1 note "the grid's outcome is
  predictable" and the E1b outline.
- **The mac's reading.** The bigger simplification is E1's chemistry, which
  has one free parameter (f_β) with every other band known exactly. Scatter
  can't turn a broad 1656 band into a narrow 1630 + 1690 pair. watford's
  candidates are on the scatter side: a size distribution, Lesson 1's
  oscillators, exact Mie, layered spheres.
- **The crux.** With fully free chemistry everything becomes non-identifiable
  trivially, since chemistry alone can fit any spectrum. So E1b needs a
  realistic, constrained chemistry prior. That is the same problem E2's
  learned prior faces (RESEARCH_BRIEF §4, "The prior's training data is the
  crux").
- Background: `docs/RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`; E1 in `docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`, including
  the fallback oscillator table and the redesign; the WORKLOG 2026-11-06
  entries.

## Tasks
1. **Candidate model changes, ranked** by how likely each is to bring the α
   and β families within reach of each other at tissue SNR, and by cost.
   - Chemistry options include:
     - free Amide I sub-band centres and widths within literature ranges (α,
       β, random coil, turns);
     - free amplitudes for the other bands;
     - mixtures of measured pure-component reference spectra.
   - Scatter options are watford's list above.
   - Done when each candidate has its parameter ranges and a citation (DOI or
     arXiv ID, checked) for the ranges.
2. **Define the target when chemistry is free.** "α vs β" must become a
   region of chemistry space, such as a secondary-structure fraction, not a
   single f_β. Propose a definition, and the wrong-mode metric that goes with
   it.
   - Done when it is stated precisely enough to code.
3. **The screening rule.** Each candidate is first screened with watford's
   degeneracy check (min χ² → critical SNR, about 4 min each). Propose the
   critical-SNR level at which a candidate earns a posterior grid, with the
   reasoning (for example, relative to tissue SNR or E0's p10 noise).
4. **A DRAFT E1b pre-registration** in the E1 format: question, model, priors,
   grid, metrics, positive and negative controls, pass/fail thresholds,
   budget.
   - Note the lesson from E1: each control must be checkable apart from the
     hypothesis it guards (E1's first positive control wasn't).
